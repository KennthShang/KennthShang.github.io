    <h2 id="phabox2-title">PhaBOX2</h2>
    <p>PhaBOX2 is a web server for discovering and characterizing viral sequences in metagenomic data. It brings viral identification, taxonomic classification, and protein annotation together in one interface.</p>
    <p>The project was published in <cite>Nucleic Acids Research</cite> in 2026.</p>
